# Changelog

## 0.2.0, 12 Nov 2026

Corrections to the first print packages.

- **Front plate sits on the lip.** In 0.1.0 the flange of the front plate stood 2 mm in front of the lip, with gaps of up to 3.8 mm, so it could not be glued. The flange now follows the real front face of the lip with a gap of about 0.5 mm (under 0.7 mm over 90 % of the lip). It touches the lip by at most 0.1 mm in a few spots.
- **Front plate re-split.** Because the rim grew, the plate is now 10 parts on the 350 × 320 bed and 16 parts on the 256 bed. All plate seams were checked against each other in the assembled position and are clear.
- **Full model added.** `full-model/` holds the unsplit back shell, front plate and formed inlay, all in the same position.
- **Renamed** to MRS-01 Modular Recording Shell. Every file starts with `MRS-01_`.
- **Overlap joints:** a small detached tongue remnant on one front plate part was removed.

## 0.1.0, 12 Nov 2026

First print packages.

- Back shell and front plate split for 256 × 256 × 256 mm and 350 × 320 × 325 mm beds.
- Three connection types: block pins, butterfly keys, overlap.
- Parts turned into their print orientation by script, for low height and little overhang.
- Stand mount: ruthex 3/8"-16 heat-set insert, from below, flush with a flat 44 mm seat. The top of the load area stays closed.
- Thin struts thickened into knuckles of about 20 mm where a cut crosses them.
- Inlay A3: one 30 mm sheet with 6 darts, edge tucked under the lip.
